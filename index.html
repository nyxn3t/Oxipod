Oxipod

Put music on your old iPod from Linux. This is Oxipod's own Flatpak repository: add it once, and new releases show up in your software center and with flatpak update.

flatpak remote-add --user --if-not-exists oxipod https://nyxn3t.github.io/Oxipod/oxipod.flatpakrepo
flatpak install --user oxipod io.github.nyxn3t.Oxipod

Or open oxipod.flatpakref with your software center.

This is not Flathub. Adding the repository means you trust me to ship updates; they are signed with this key: 899048DA42D4FA507EC6F01FA9A4E497D0B17C45.

Oxipod on GitHub: the source, the releases with a plain .flatpak file, and the place to say how it works on your iPod.
